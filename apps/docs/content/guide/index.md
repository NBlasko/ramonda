# Start here

Two pages, in this order. Together they take about twenty minutes and leave you with a component
that holds state and answers a click.

- **[Installation](/guide/installation)** — a new project with `create-ramonda`, or Ramonda added to
  one you already have. The decorators need a build step, and this is where that is set up.
- **[Your first component](/guide/first-component)** — a class, a `@state` field, a handler. The
  smallest thing that is really a component rather than a snippet.

## What to read after those

The order below is the one the site is written in, and each answers a question the one before it
raises.

- **[Components](/concepts/components)** and **[JSX](/concepts/jsx)** — what you are actually
  writing, and what it puts on the page.
- **[State](/concepts/state)** and **[Props](/concepts/props)** — where a value lives, and how one
  travels from a parent.
- **[Events](/concepts/events)** — the handlers that change it.

By then a page works. **[Lifecycle](/concepts/lifecycle)**, **[derived
values](/concepts/compute)** and **[lists](/lists)** are what it needs to grow.

## If you have used a framework before

Two pages are worth reading early, because they explain choices that otherwise look arbitrary:
**[why a class](/why/classes)** and **[the reactivity model](/why/reactivity)**. Ramonda has no
hooks-with-rules and no virtual-DOM diffing of your whole tree, and both pages say what it does
instead.
