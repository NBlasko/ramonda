## What was NOT taken from anywhere

Two things in this repository look like they might carry an obligation and do not. Both are written
down because a reader would otherwise have to work it out.

**Browser engine data.** `@ramonda/css` holds lists of vendor-prefixed properties and of keywords
each engine accepts. No engine's source was read. The lists are produced by LAUNCHING Chromium,
Firefox and WebKit and asking them — `CSS.supports(property, value)`, `getComputedStyle`, and
enumerating `CSSStyleDeclaration`'s own prototype. That is a measurement of what a running program
does, and the result is our own record of it. See `scripts/build-engine-keywords.mjs` and
`scripts/build-prefixed-properties.mjs`.

**The editor grammars.** `tools/vscode-css` injects into TypeScript and CSS by REFERENCE —
`"include": "source.css#property-values"` and its neighbours — rather than embedding a copy of
either grammar. Six patterns of its own, and nothing anybody else wrote.
